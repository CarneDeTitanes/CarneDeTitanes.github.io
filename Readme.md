Purpose & context

Javier is the Dungeon Master for a long-term homebrew D&D 5e campaign called "Carne de Titanes", set in a world called El Suelo, centered on the city of Piedra Hueca. The campaign is a sequel to "Mazmorra de los 10 Pisos," set 200 years later. The dominant worldbuilding element is ten god-tier entities called Colosales, which dominate regions through fear, armies, and psychological control — they need not be physically giant. Belial, the most powerful, died in the prior campaign; the Gran Avatar (the living dungeon beneath Piedra Hueca) absorbed enough power from his death to become a Coloso itself — effectively the 10th. The other Colosales avoid Piedra Hueca due to territorial dynamics between Colosos, not residual fear of Belial. The Gran Avatar also blocks psychic intrusion, protecting characters inside the city but not outside it.

La Ciudadela appearing in character sheets refers only to the homebrew rulebook ("La Ciudadela: El Nuevo Mundo"), not a faction. In-world, "La Ciudadela" is flavored as an ancient ruined city, once the continent's capital — unrelated to Cinto's narrative.

Javier's preferred working style: submit raw notes → Claude infers structure, adds GM-facing guidance, delivers polished copy-paste-ready output. He wants proactive flagging of worldbuilding inconsistencies and balance concerns before content is drafted. Communication is in Spanish. He handles encounter design and balance calculations himself and does not want Claude doing those unless explicitly asked.

Key learnings & principles

Cosmological consistency: The Colosales structure is the load-bearing element of the setting. All imported published content (Strahd, Auril, etc.) must be reflavored to fit the Coloso model or its cosmological conflicts flagged before integration.
Two-rulebook principle: "La Ciudadela: El Nuevo Mundo" and "Espiral de Secretos de Valda" (both Nivel20) are used exclusively as mechanical content sources — races, classes, feats, spells, bestiary, equipment. No lore, setting, factions, or narrative from either book (including La Ciudadela's Cataclismo/Velo/Vacío cosmology, and Valda's firearms/monstrous grafts as narrative hooks) enters the campaign world.
PG system: The unified guild uses a single shared PG pool. Level thresholds: 0, 30, 60, 105, 150, 195, 255, 315, 375, 435, 510, 585, 660, 735, 825, 915, 1005, 1095, 1200, 1305. Rewards: Oneshot Fácil 8 / Medio 12 / Difícil 16; Parte de Campaña Medio 35 / Duro 45; Derrota de Coloso 280–420. Modifiers: casi pierde PJ −2, pierde PJ −8, objetivo secundario +3, roleplay excepcional +2, desventaja notable +4, fallo obj. principal ×0.5, bonus completar campaña +50. Designed for ~1 level per 2 sessions early, scaling to ~1 level per 7 sessions near level 20.
Character arc timing: Personal arcs are not developed proactively — only when something significant happens in the campaign or Javier explicitly raises the topic.
Encounter design: Javier uses his own homebrew balancing formula. Claude does not design encounters or calculate balance unless specifically asked.
Flagging before drafting: Worldbuilding inconsistencies and continuity concerns should be raised before producing content, not after.
